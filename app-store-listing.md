## Description

The wait is almost over.

GTA6 News is a fan-made timer counting down to the biggest launch night of the year: Thursday, November 19, 2026, at midnight Eastern.

Open the app and ride through a neon-soaked loading show — original sunset artwork, palm silhouettes and harbor lights — then watch the big clock tick down live: days, hours, minutes and seconds, plus your total hours left and a "final stretch" meter for the last 30 days.

When the clock hits zero, it's launch night: the app celebrates with you.

FEATURES
• Live countdown to Thursday, November 19, 2026 — midnight Eastern
• A launch news feed with the latest official posts, headlines and videos — tap any card to read the original story
• Original neon sunset artwork — no screenshots, no clips, all original
• One-tap "Add Launch Night to Calendar" so you never miss the moment
• Launch night facts and a get-ready checklist (charge the controller, clear the schedule, stock the snacks)
• A launch-night celebration the second the timer hits zero
• After launch, the timer counts UP — see exactly how long the game has been out, with Day 1, Week 1 and Month 1 milestones
• The countdown itself works fully offline — the whole show is built into the app (the news feed and ads load when you're connected)

GTA6 News is an unofficial, fan-made app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any game publisher or developer.
